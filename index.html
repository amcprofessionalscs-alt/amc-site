Questions GCs ask

What does a construction final clean include?

Dust removal from ceilings, vents, ledges and fixtures, top down. Streak-free glass, mirrors and storefronts. Protective film and labels removed. Floors scrubbed with the grout lines cleared. Restrooms detailed fixture by fixture.

Which areas does AMC Professionals serve?

Construction final cleans and commercial cleaning in the Milwaukee, Phoenix and Chicago areas. AMC is based in Wauwatosa, Wisconsin.

Is AMC Professionals licensed and insured?

Yes. AMC Professionals LLC is licensed and insured, and has been operating since 2020.
